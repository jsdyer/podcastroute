Your workspace, your device

Your follows, queue, notes and playback positions save automatically in this browser. Turn saving off for a temporary tab, or export a backup to move to another browser or device.

What leaves your browser? Search terms, selected podcast IDs and imported feed URLs go to CoolCode’s API service and Podcast Index. Playing an episode or loading cover art contacts its publisher. Your follows, queue, notes and listening progress save in this browser by default and are never uploaded by this app. Clearing browser data or using a private window can remove them; export a backup to keep another copy.
